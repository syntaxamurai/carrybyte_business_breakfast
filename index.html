Why this breakfast matters

Your organisation has backups.
But can it actually recover?

A backup that has never been successfully restored is an assumption— not a proven recovery strategy. This breakfast examines the gap between having copies and being able to restore critical operations.

01 — RECOVERY TESTING

Untested restores create false confidence.

A successful backup job does not prove that the business can recover.

02 — INDEPENDENCE

Your backup may share the same risk.

One site, credential domain or failure mode can undermine both production and recovery.

03 — RECOVERY OBJECTIVES

RTOs and RPOs must reflect the business.

Critical workloads should have recovery objectives based on operational impact.

04 — OWNERSHIP

Recovery needs a decision owner.

Runbooks, escalation paths and named decision-makers matter during an incident.
