Why business resilience matters now.

Your organisation has backups.
But can it recover when the worst happens?

Ransomware, infrastructure failure, political disruption, cloud account compromise, accidental deletion and other incidents can make critical data unavailable. This breakfast examines whether your recovery strategy can withstand the events most likely to put your operations at risk.

01 Ransomware & cyberattacks

Your backup must survive the attack too.

Ransomware can target production systems, backup repositories and credentials. Recovery needs independent, protected copies and proven restores.

02 System & infrastructure failure

One failure should not take recovery with it.

Hardware, software, power, network, storage or cloud failures can make production unavailable — and expose weaknesses in recovery.

03 Political & physical risk

Your recovery plan must survive loss of site or access.

Political disruption, civil unrest, fire, flood or loss of physical access can affect both on-premise infrastructure and recovery sites.

04 Recovery readiness

Recovery needs to be independent, tested and owned.

Runbooks, escalation paths, protected credentials and named decision-makers matter when normal access is no longer available.
